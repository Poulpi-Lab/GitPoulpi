libgit2 (statically linked, unmodified)
  GitPoulpi's libgit2 backend links libgit2 1.8.1, compiled from the sources
  that the libgit2-sys crate bundles, without any change. libgit2 is released
  under GPLv2 with a linking exception, which allows linking unmodified
  copies from any program. Source: https://github.com/libgit2/libgit2 (tag
  v1.8.1) and https://crates.io/crates/libgit2-sys . Its COPYING file, which
  also carries the notices of the components bundled in it (PCRE, SHA1DC,
  llhttp, wildmatch, RFC 6234 SHA-256 and others), is reproduced below.

syntect (syntax highlighting)
  The syntect crate (Tristan Hume and contributors, MIT, see section 4) embeds
  compiled grammar and theme dumps. The syntax definitions come from the
  Sublime Text "Packages" repository (https://github.com/sublimehq/Packages),
  whose packages carry their own permissive licenses (many derive from
  TextMate bundles). The color themes it bundles, each MIT licensed under the
  permission notice reproduced at the end of the credits (section 1):
    - base16-ocean (dark, light), base16-eighties and base16-mocha: the Base16
      schemes of Chris Kempson (https://github.com/chriskempson/base16), as
      packaged in the Spacegray package, Copyright (c) Sublime Text Packages
      (https://github.com/SublimeText/Spacegray);
    - Solarized (dark, light): colors by Ethan Schoonover, as packaged by Koen
      Lageveen, Copyright (c) 2016 Koen Lageveen
      (https://github.com/braver/Solarized);
    - InspiredGitHub: Copyright (c) 2015 Seth Lopez
      (https://github.com/sethlopez/InspiredGitHub.tmtheme).
  The provenance of each is documented in the syntect repository:
  https://github.com/trishume/syntect

webpki-roots (root certificates)
  The webpki-roots crate embeds the Mozilla CA root list, provided under the
  Community Data License Agreement - Permissive, Version 2.0 (CDLA-
  Permissive-2.0). Its text is reproduced with the crate licenses in section 4
  (see "webpki-roots").
