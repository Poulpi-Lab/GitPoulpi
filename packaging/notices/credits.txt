GitPoulpi ships color presets. Only color values (facts about a palette) are
used, never code or files from these projects. Each family is credited here
with the license its repository declares and the copyright holder named in
its license file (verified against each upstream repository, 2026-10-26).

Lane (graph line) palettes
  "Material": a selection of 16 of the 400 shades of the Material Design
    color palette (Google), unchanged and in GitPoulpi's own order,
    https://m2.material.io/design/color ; the same values are published in
    Material Design Lite (Copyright 2015 Google Inc, Apache-2.0, license text
    in section 4), https://github.com/google/material-design-lite
  "Serie": branch colors of lusingander/serie, MIT
    https://github.com/lusingander/serie
  The other lane palettes are GitPoulpi's own or follow the theme they are
  named after.
